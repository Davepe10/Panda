# Real pet audio sources

Pandalandia 60 does not ship the old synthesized WAV pack. Pet audio is streamed from original public/Creative Commons recordings at runtime.

- Bark: **Ladrido perro.ogg**, Wikimedia Commons, Edo.pt2, CC0 1.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ladrido_perro.ogg
- Happy/play/pant source: **Perro feliz.ogg**, Wikimedia Commons, Armartinell, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Perro_feliz.ogg
- Lick/shake/natural dog source: **Dog.ogg**, Wikimedia Commons / PDSounds, soerena, Public Domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dog.ogg
- Eat source: **Canemangia.ogv**, Wikimedia Commons, Calogero Bonaventura.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canemangia.ogv
- Drink source: **Dog Drinking in Slow Motion (Berger Blanc Suisse called Bonny).ogv**, Wikimedia Commons, Sebastian Hornbostel, CC BY-SA.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dog_Drinking_in_Slow_Motion_(Berger_Blanc_Suisse_called_Bonny).ogv

The local synthesized bark/sniff/pant/lick/eat/drink/play/shake WAV files from earlier versions were removed.
