# Pandalandia V67 — Responsive + Pet Care Hardening

- Responsive hardening for phone, tablet, laptop and desktop; Jugar no overflows horizontally.
- Six stable pet-care actions are always available: food, water, rest, bath, play and pet. Low needs are highlighted instead of replacing actions.
- Food/water bowls are directly interactive inside the 3D room.
- Cocker approaches the bowl during eating/drinking behaviors.
- Grounding corrected by growth stage/type to prevent paws rendering under the floor.
- Head wearables lowered and synchronized with locomotion to remove floating accessory appearance.
- Bath feedback/bubbles added without changing persistence/RPC behavior.
- Stronger global contrast rules on cards and body copy while preserving hero text.
- Mobile bottom safe area spacing increased so navigation does not cover content.

No database migration is required from V66.
